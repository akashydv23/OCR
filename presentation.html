⚠️ The Core Dilemma

Why Indian Language OCR is Broken

Digitizing vernacular legal, historical, and governance records forced an unacceptable trade-off between security and accuracy.

🚨 Crisis 1: The Privacy Vulnerability

Standard OCR requires sending files to third-party cloud servers.

  • Courts & lawyers cannot risk uploading confidential client records.
  • Citizens risk leaking Aadhaar, PAN, and banking documents.
  • Zero offline functionality in remote districts or low-bandwidth areas.

🧩 Crisis 2: Script & Matra Degradation

Indic scripts use non-linear glyphs, matras, and conjuncts.

  • Standard engines detach vowel signs (जैसे 'ि' या 'ु') from root consonants.
  • Complex conjuncts (प्र, ष्ट्र, ज्ञ) become unreadable symbols.
  • Cloud LLMs frequently hallucinate or translate instead of verbatim transcription.

🛠️ Engineering War Stories

What Broke, and How We Got Out

Pushing browser standards to their limits brought severe runtime bottlenecks. Here is how we solved them:

💥 Browser OOM Crashes

The Break: 20+ page 300 DPI PDFs pushed JS heap over 1.5GB, crashing browser tabs.

The Fix: Engineered a virtual streaming page pipeline, downscaling idle canvases and paging image blobs to IndexedDB (Dexie.js). RAM stayed <60MB.

✂️ Broken Ligatures & Matras

The Break: OCR severed dependent vowel matras (like 'ि') from parent consonants.

The Fix: Built CrossValidationAgent with Unicode regex heuristics to reconstruct orphaned grapheme clusters post-OCR.

🎯 PDF Searchable Drift

The Break: Invisible text highlights drifted away from original scan words on copy-paste.

The Fix: Mapped raw bounding boxes to PDF points via affine transform matrices with pdf-lib text render mode 3.
